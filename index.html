Our Story

Born from a Love of Coffee & Community

Moka & Bloom was born in 2018 when two friends — a barista and a pastry chef — decided that Mumbai deserved a café that felt like home. Not just another coffee shop, but a space where conversations linger, ideas bloom, and every cup is crafted with intention.

We source our beans directly from small farms in Coorg and Chikmagalur, working closely with growers who share our belief that great coffee starts long before it reaches the cup. Every roast is done in-house, in small batches, to ensure freshness and flavour.

Our kitchen follows the same philosophy — seasonal ingredients, honest flavours, and nothing you can't pronounce. We bake fresh every morning and change the menu with the seasons.
